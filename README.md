## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🏥 MediConnect

**Digital Healthcare Management Platform**

A full-stack healthcare ecosystem designed to connect **patients, doctors, pharmacists, pharmacies, hospitals, and health authorities** through a unified digital platform.

**Key Features**
- 🔐 JWT Authentication & Role-Based Access Control
- 👨‍⚕️ Doctor availability and appointment management
- 📋 Electronic healthcare records and patient consent
- 💊 Digital prescriptions and pharmacy dispensing
- 📦 Pharmacy inventory management
- 🪪 Digital Health ID & QR patient lookup
- 🤖 AI-powered healthcare assistant
- 🛡️ Audit logging and protected healthcare workflows

**Tech:** `React` • `FastAPI` • `PostgreSQL` • `Docker` • `Gemini API`
